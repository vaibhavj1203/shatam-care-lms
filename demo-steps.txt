SHATAM CARE LMS - GUIDED DEMO
A 15-minute walkthrough that SHOWS the product working. Everything is already
set up for you by the seeder, so you start by using the app, not building it.

(If you want to test every feature and edge case instead of demoing, use
 test-steps.txt - that is the thorough QA script. This file is for showing
 someone what the product does.)


SET UP ONCE (about 15 min the first time, under a minute after)
  cd frappe
  docker compose up -d
  cd shatam-care-web && npm run dev

  Then load the demo scenario - run this from the frappe folder (it talks to
  the backend on :8000, so the stack above must be up first):
  python3 seed-demo.py

  This creates a ready-to-show training course, a learner already enrolled in
  it, and the staff accounts below. Everything is at http://localhost:3000.
  Safe to re-run - it reuses existing accounts and resets their passwords.

  THE LOGINS
    Administrator               / admin                  (runs everything)
    demo.student@example.com    / Kestrel-Bamboo-42       (a caregiver learning)
    demo.evaluator@example.com  / Evaluator-Bamboo-42     (approves certificates only)
    demo.coordinator@example.com/ Coord-Bamboo-42         (a full coordinator)

  Sign out between roles, or use a separate private window per role so you can
  flip between them without logging in and out.


====================================================================
 ACT 1 - WHAT A CAREGIVER EXPERIENCES   (log in as demo.student)
====================================================================
The learner is a caregiver in a tier-3 town, often on a phone, learning from
video. The product's job is to make sure they actually watched and understood -
not just left the video playing.

1.  Log in as demo.student@example.com / Kestrel-Bamboo-42.
    You land on a simple dashboard: My Courses, My Certificates. Nothing else -
    a learner is never shown admin machinery.

2.  Open "My Courses" -> "Demo: Safe Patient Handling" -> open the lesson
    "Lifting a patient safely". The video starts playing.

3.  >>> THE SIGNATURE FEATURE <<<
    About 5 seconds in, the video STOPS on its own and a question appears on
    top of it: "When lifting a patient, what should you bend?"
    The learner cannot skip ahead or carry on until they answer. This is how
    the platform evidences real viewing in place of a classroom register.

4.  Choose "Your knees" and Submit. It confirms "Correct!", you click
    Continue watching, and the video resumes exactly where it paused.

5.  Show that it respects the learner's time: reload the page and play again.
    The question does NOT interrupt a second time - checkpoints only enforce on
    the first watch, so revising a lesson later is never punished.

6.  Let the video run to the end. It marks the lesson complete on its own.

7.  Back on the course, click "Take assessment". Answer the final question
    (the back is the body part safe lifting protects), submit.
    It says you passed - and that your certificate is now AWAITING APPROVAL.
    Note what just happened: finishing the videos is not enough. A human still
    has to sign off before a government-recognised certificate is issued.


====================================================================
 ACT 2 - MANY LANGUAGES, ONE VIDEO   (show the multilingual angle)
====================================================================
Shatam trains across Hindi, Marathi and more. The platform translates the
on-screen quiz text per language without duplicating the course.

8.  In a second window, log in as demo.coordinator@example.com / Coord-Bamboo-42.
    Click Content -> open "Lifting a patient safely" -> find the checkpoint ->
    under "Translations" choose a language (e.g. Hindi), type the translated
    question and options, and click "Save translation".

9.  Back as demo.student, open the course page and change the Language dropdown
    to Hindi. Re-open the lesson and reach the checkpoint.
    The question now appears in Hindi - same video, same lesson, learner's own
    language. (The video's own audio is handled by YouTube's dubbed tracks.)


====================================================================
 ACT 3 - THE HUMAN SIGN-OFF & A VERIFIABLE CERTIFICATE
====================================================================
10. Log in as demo.evaluator@example.com / Evaluator-Bamboo-42.
    Notice the top bar shows ONLY "Approvals" - nothing else. This person was
    trusted with exactly one job and the interface reflects precisely that.

11. Click Approvals. The demo student is listed as eligible and pending.
    Click "Approve & issue certificate". Done - that is the final gate.

12. Log back in as demo.student -> Certificates.
    There is now a certificate with an ID like SCF-2026-000001.

13. >>> VERIFIABLE BY ANYONE <<<
    Click "Verification page". It shows "Valid Certificate" with the learner's
    name, course and date. Now copy that URL into a private/logged-out window -
    it STILL resolves. An employer can confirm a caregiver's credential with no
    account and no login. Each certificate also carries a QR code to the same
    page.

14. Click "Download PDF" to show the printable certificate itself.


====================================================================
 ACT 4 - HOW AN ADMIN RUNS IT   (log in as Administrator)
====================================================================
This is the "and it is built for how you actually operate" close.

15. Log in as Administrator / admin. The dashboard shows the full set of tools:
    Courses, Content, People, Groups, Review, Approvals.

16. Click People. Every account carries a persona badge, and for evaluators you
    can see exactly which of the five rights they hold:
        Manage courses | Author content | Review content
        Approve certificates | Manage people
    Click "Edit rights" on someone to show rights are granted and revoked per
    person - "Evaluator" is whatever you delegate, nothing more. That is why
    demo.evaluator saw only Approvals a moment ago.

17. Open any course. Show that a course can carry MULTIPLE teachers and MULTIPLE
    certificate evaluators (the "Teachers" and "Certificate Evaluators"
    sections) - so approvals are shared across a team of coordinators and a
    course is never stuck waiting on one person.

18. Click Groups. Student Groups are internal tags - by region or field
    coordinator (e.g. "Nashik - Coordinator Priya") - for reporting. Learners
    never see them.

19. (Optional) Click Content to show the authoring side: the interactive video
    scrubber where a teacher places those pause-to-answer checkpoints on the
    timeline, and submits a lesson for admin review before it goes live.


====================================================================
 THE ONE-LINE PITCH, IF YOU NEED IT
====================================================================
Curated video training for caregivers, where the platform proves they watched
and understood (pause-to-answer checkpoints), tests them, has a human approve
them, and issues a certificate anyone can verify online - in the learner's own
language, run by a small team with finely delegated permissions.


WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT HERE YET  (be upfront if asked)
  - No self-service signup - an admin creates every account today.
  - Branding is placeholder: green colours, no logo, stock certificate design.
  - Final-assessment questions can't yet be translated from the UI (the in-video
    checkpoints can, as shown in Act 2).


WHEN DONE
  docker compose stop        (keeps everything; "start" brings it back in seconds)
  Do NOT use "docker compose down" - it discards the running setup.
