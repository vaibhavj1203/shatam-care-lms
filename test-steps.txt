SHATAM CARE LMS - MANUAL TEST STEPS
Everything is at http://localhost:3000
Sign out between roles, or use a private window.

LOGINS
  admin      Administrator                / admin
  teacher    demo.teacher@example.com     / Teacher-Bamboo-42
  evaluator  demo.evaluator@example.com   / Evaluator-Bamboo-42
  student    demo.student@example.com     / Kestrel-Bamboo-42

BEFORE YOU START
  cd frappe
  docker compose up -d
  cd shatam-care-web && npm run dev
  cd .. && python3 seed-demo.py


PART 1 - STUDENT
1.  Go to localhost:3000. Log in as student.
    See: only "Courses" and "Certificates" in the top bar. No admin links.
2.  Click Courses.
    See: a list of course cards.
3.  Open "Demo: Safe Patient Handling".
    See: the real course title as the heading, one chapter with one lesson,
         a Language dropdown, and a Final Assessment card saying pass mark 50%.
4.  Open the lesson "Lifting a patient safely".
    See: the video player loads and plays.
5.  Wait until 0:05.  <-- MOST IMPORTANT STEP
    See: video PAUSES and a question appears over it.
6.  Choose "Your knees", click Submit answer.
    See: "Correct!"
7.  Click Continue watching.
    See: overlay closes, video RESUMES.
8.  Reload the page and play again from the start.
    See: the question does NOT appear a second time.
9.  Let the video play to the end.
    See: "Lesson marked complete".
10. Go back to the course. Click Take assessment.
    See: one question, header says pass mark 50%.
11. Choose "Your back", click Submit assessment.
    See: "You passed!" and a note about evaluator approval.
12. Go back to the course page.
    See: Certificate section says awaiting evaluator approval.
13. Change the Language dropdown.
    See: "Saved" appears.

PART 2 - EVALUATOR
14. Sign out. Log in as evaluator.
    See: only "Approvals" in the top bar.
15. Click Approvals.
    See: the student listed as pending.
16. Click "Approve & issue certificate".
    See: the row disappears.

PART 3 - STUDENT COLLECTS CERTIFICATE
17. Sign out. Log in as student. Click Certificates.
    See: a certificate with an ID like SCF-2026-000006.
18. Click "Verification page".
    See: "Valid Certificate" with name, course and date.
19. Copy that URL into a private window (logged out).
    See: still shows "Valid Certificate".
20. "Download PDF" is known broken. Skip it.

PART 4 - TEACHER
21. Sign out. Log in as teacher. Click My Lessons.
    See: the demo course with its lesson marked Approved.
22. Click "+ Add lesson". Chapter: Module 1 - Foundations.
    Title: anything. YouTube: dQw4w9WgXcQ. Create.
    See: new lesson appears with a Draft badge.
23. Open your new lesson.
    See: the video player loads.
24. Let it play a few seconds, click "Pause here & add quiz checkpoint".
    See: video pauses, a form appears with that timestamp.
25. Type a question, fill both options, tick the correct one, Save checkpoint.
    See: a green marker appears on the bar under the video.
26. Click the timestamp next to the checkpoint.
    See: the video jumps to that point.
27. Click Edit on the checkpoint, change the question, Save changes.
    See: the new wording in the list.
28. Click Delete on that checkpoint.
    See: it disappears (allowed, since no student has answered it).
29. Click "Submit for review".
    See: status becomes Submitted for Review.

PART 5 - ADMIN
30. Sign out. Log in as admin.
    See: all eight cards on the dashboard.
31. Click Review.
    See: the teacher's lesson from step 29. Click Approve. It disappears.
32. Click Manage Courses.
    See: courses with lesson and enrolment counts, Published/Draft badges.
        Courses missing an evaluator or assessment are flagged in amber.
33. Click "+ New course", give it a title, Create. Then open it.
34. Under Teachers, search "demo.teacher" and add them.
    See: they appear in the list.
35. Under Certificate Evaluator, search "demo.evaluator" and select them.
    See: "Currently: demo.evaluator@example.com".
36. Under Final Assessment, set a pass mark, click Create.
    Then add a question with two options, tick the correct one, Add question.
    See: the question appears in the list.
37. Click Publish.
    See: badge changes to Published.
38. Click Students, then "+ New student".
    First try password demo1234 - it should be REJECTED as too weak.
    Then register someone properly and enrol them in your new course.
    See: a temporary password shown once. Write it down.
39. Click Groups. Create a group, e.g. "Nashik - Coordinator Priya".
    See: it appears in the list.
40. Sign out. Log in as the student you made in step 38.
    Open your new course and click Enrol.
    See: enrolment works and the lessons become clickable.


IF SOMETHING FAILS
  Note the step number and what you saw instead.
  Press F12, check the Console tab for red errors.
  Press F12, check the Network tab for failed (red) requests.
  Backend logs: docker compose logs --tail 100 frappe

KNOWN GAPS - NOT BUGS
  There is no signup page. Students must be created by an admin (step 38).
  Download PDF does not work (step 20).
  Branding is placeholder - green colours, no logo, stock certificate design.

WHEN DONE
  docker compose stop
  Do not use "docker compose down" - it deletes the setup inside the container.
